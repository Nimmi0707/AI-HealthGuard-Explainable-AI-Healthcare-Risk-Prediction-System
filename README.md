## 📌 About the Project

**AI HealthGuard** is an academic AI research prototype that demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence and Explainable AI (XAI) can be used for healthcare risk analysis.

The system analyzes health-related input data and presents potential risk predictions along with understandable explanations of the factors contributing to the prediction.

The project focuses on making AI-based healthcare analysis more **transparent, interpretable, and user-friendly**.

## 🧠 Explainable AI

A major focus of AI HealthGuard is **Explainable Artificial Intelligence**.

Instead of presenting only a risk prediction, the system is designed to provide understandable insights into the factors that contribute to the prediction.

The prototype demonstrates concepts such as:

- Feature importance
- Risk-contributing factors
- Prediction confidence
- Explainable prediction results
- AI-assisted recommendations

This helps demonstrate how AI systems can become more interpretable for users.

---

## 🏥 Risk Assessment

The system provides a structured healthcare risk assessment interface where users can enter relevant health and lifestyle information.

The prototype processes the provided information and presents the corresponding risk analysis through an interactive dashboard.

### Risk Categories

- 🔴 High Risk
- 🟠 Medium Risk
- 🟢 Low Risk

> These categories are part of the academic prototype and should not be interpreted as actual medical diagnoses.

The application provides an interactive dashboard containing:

## 💡 AI-Based Recommendations

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of AI HealthGuard is to develop an intelligent and interpretable healthcare risk analysis prototype that demonstrates how AI can assist in analyzing health-related data while making prediction results easier to understand.

The project focuses on the following objectives:

1. Develop an AI-powered healthcare risk assessment interface.
2. Demonstrate explainable AI concepts.
3. Visualize healthcare risk patterns and analytics.
4. Provide understandable explanations for AI predictions.
5. Demonstrate AI-assisted recommendations.
6. Build a modern and responsive healthcare AI interface.
7. Explore the role of AI in transparent healthcare data analysis.
